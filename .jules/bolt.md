## 2026-10-25 - [Optimize SQLite batch insert IDs mapping]
**Learning:** Python's `sqlite3` driver resets `cur.lastrowid` to `None` when using `executemany()`. To capture auto-incremented IDs without `RETURNING` support when doing batch inserts, `SELECT last_insert_rowid()` executed immediately after `executemany()` works correctly even if the table has `AFTER INSERT` triggers. The previous inserted IDs in the batch can then be reliably calculated backward.
**Action:** When converting looped `.execute()` statements to `.executemany()` for optimization, retrieve IDs using `SELECT last_insert_rowid()` and subtract the batch length to determine the full ID range instead of relying on `cur.lastrowid` or individual insertions. Additionally, applying a microsecond offset `(i * 1e-6)` to timestamps ensures they remain unique per row inside the batch.
