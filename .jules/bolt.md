## 2026-06-25 - [SQLite Batch Insert Row IDs Optimization]
**Learning:** Python's `sqlite3` driver sets `cur.lastrowid` to `None` after `executemany()`. A common anti-pattern to get these IDs is looping `conn.execute("INSERT ...")` for every row, which introduces high I/O overhead. However, AFTER INSERT triggers on main tables do not interfere with the connection's `last_insert_rowid()` result.
**Action:** To retrieve auto-incremented IDs for batch inserts without a `RETURNING` clause safely, execute `SELECT last_insert_rowid()` immediately after the `conn.executemany(...)` call. Then calculate the preceding inserted IDs backward using `cur.rowcount` or `len(batch)`. For this, ensure that rows generate strictly unique identifiers such as constraints on timestamps `ts = base_time + (i * 1e-6)`.
